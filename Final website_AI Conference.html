About the Conference

This conference focuses on gaining communal insight into the coming developments in artificial intelligence, and on learning together how to deal with them and move forward. Our aim is to help our community strengthen itself against the challenges these developments bring.

Call for Papers

We call for papers grounded in the spirit of togetherness. Research papers are welcome but not mandatory; we equally invite well-reasoned opinions on how literature can move forward, with AI or without it. We are especially interested in how publishing careers and the creative field are changing alongside AI, and what can be done in response.

How do we accept or reject the presence of AI in literature, and what can we do to move forward responsibly?

Key Questions

  • What defines authorship and authenticity as AI writing approaches a style indistinguishable from human writing?
    • How can we navigate and distinguish human writing from AI, and what techniques can we employ to establish human writing as an art form?
  • How is the field of literature changing, and what will literature be in 10 years?
  • What are the real dangers and opportunities AI presents to literary practice?
  • How should AI be regulated in academic writing versus creative fiction? Should different rules apply?
  • Can AI ever truly create literature, or is it fundamentally a tool?
