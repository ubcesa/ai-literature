The Future of AI in Literature

An international conference hosted by the English Students' Association at the University of British Columbia.

Hybrid format: in person in Vancouver, Canada, and online.

Submit an Abstract Read the Call for Papers

About the Conference

Artificial intelligence is changing how we read, write, publish and create. This conference brings students and scholars together to respond to that change as a community.

We see AI as a shared concern that is best understood through open conversation across universities and disciplines. Our aim is to build knowledge together and leave our field better prepared for what comes next.

Call for Papers

This year's conference is guided by the idea of togetherness. We invite contributions on how writers, scholars and creative practitioners can respond to the growing presence of AI. We are especially interested in how AI has affected publishing careers and creative work, and what can be done to protect and support them. You may argue for working with AI, against it, or somewhere in between.

How do we accept or reject the presence of AI in literature, and what can we do to move forward responsibly?

Possible topics include

  • Changes to publishing careers since AI tools entered the industry
  • Authorship and authenticity when AI can produce human-like writing
  • The risks and opportunities AI brings to literary and creative practice
  • Whether AI should be treated differently in academic and creative writing
  • How literary communities can support one another, with or without AI

Submissions

What we accept

Research papers are welcome but not required. We also accept position papers, critical reflections and opinion pieces.

Who can submit

Students from any university and any year of study, and anyone studying or interested in English literature.

Guidelines

A Note on Evidence

All submissions, including opinion pieces, should be based on accurate and verifiable information. Please support your arguments with facts and sources, so that everyone leaves the conference with strong ideas and correct information.
